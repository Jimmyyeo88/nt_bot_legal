# Privacy Policy — NT Signal Bridge

Last updated: 2026-10-18

1. **Who we are.** NT Signal Bridge ("the Bot") is operated by Jimmy Yeo ("the Operator"). Contact: open an issue at <https://github.com/Jimmyyeo88/nt_bot_legal/issues>.
2. **Data we collect.** The Bot only sends messages. It does not read message content and does not request privileged intents. It processes only the IDs of the server and channels it is installed in.
3. **Data we do not collect.** We do not collect message content, direct messages, email addresses, payment information, or location.
4. **How we use data.** Only to deliver notifications and operate the Bot. We do not sell, rent or share data with third parties, and do not use it for advertising or profiling.
5. **Storage and retention.** Operational logs may contain server and channel IDs and are kept for up to 30 days on the Operator's own servers, then deleted. Credentials are stored privately and never published.
6. **Third parties.** The Bot runs on Discord; Discord's own Privacy Policy applies to data Discord handles.
7. **Your rights.** You can remove the Bot at any time. To request deletion of any stored data, open an issue at the link above.
8. **Children.** The Bot is not directed at anyone under 13 (or the minimum age in your country).
9. **Changes.** Updates will change the date above.
