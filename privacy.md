# Privacy Policy — NT Signal Bridge

Last updated: 2026-10-18

1. **Who we are.** NT Signal Bridge ("the Bot") is operated by Jimmy Yeo ("the Operator"). Contact: open an issue at <https://github.com/Jimmyyeo88/nt_bot_legal/issues>.
2. **Data we collect.** The Bot reads the messages posted in one private channel (`#signals`) of the Operator's own server, because that channel carries the trading signals the Bot exists to receive. For this the Bot uses Discord's Message Content intent. It processes the content of those messages, plus the IDs of the server and channels it is installed in. It does not read any other channel.
3. **Data we do not collect.** We do not collect direct messages, messages from other channels, email addresses, payment information, or location. Signal messages are processed in memory; the Operator keeps only arrival records (message version and arrival time, used to compare delivery speed) and the operational logs described in section 5.
4. **How we use data.** Only to receive trading signals, deliver notifications and operate the Bot. We do not sell, rent or share data with third parties, and do not use it for advertising or profiling.
5. **Storage and retention.** Operational logs and arrival records may contain server and channel IDs and are kept for up to 30 days on the Operator's own servers, then deleted. Credentials are stored privately and never published.
6. **Third parties.** The Bot runs on Discord; Discord's own Privacy Policy applies to data Discord handles.
7. **Your rights.** You can remove the Bot at any time. To request deletion of any stored data, open an issue at the link above.
8. **Children.** The Bot is not directed at anyone under 13 (or the minimum age in your country).
9. **Changes.** Updates will change the date above.
